Stay smart

🛡️ Squishy shopping safety

Start with real NeeDoh. Schylling warns about counterfeit stores impersonating the brand. Use its official authorized retailer directory ↗ to check retailers.

Watch for assorted colors. Some stores send a random color or design, even if the product photo shows a specific one.

Don't confuse reference price with a deal. The manufacturer's price is a benchmark, not proof that any seller has inventory at that price.

Photos: For now, illustrations are emoji placeholders. We won't use misleading or unlicensed product photos.
